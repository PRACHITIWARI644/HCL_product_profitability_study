💡 Key Strategic Findings & Recommendations

  • Electronics Dilemma: Drives 54.2% of total sales ($1.42M) but commands a slim 8.52% margin. High discounts in Q4 push multiple SKUs underwater. Recommendation: Institute a hard discount cap of 12% on high-ticket tech products.
  • Furniture Margin Crisis: Generates $314.8K revenue (12.0%) but delivers a staggering 1.34% net margin ($4,228 profit). Recommendation: Restructure supplier procurement contracts and charge logistics handling fees for bulky items.
  • High-Yield Engines (Beauty & Apparel): Beauty & Personal Care generates an astonishing 64.01% gross margin, while Apparel achieves 42.15% margin. Recommendation: Reallocate marketing budget to expand shelf-space and search visibility for these high-margin lines.
  • Digital Payments Advantage: Credit Card and UPI orders demonstrate significantly higher Average Order Values ($340+) and faster settlement times compared to Cash on Delivery.
